SubZona

Traditional rental apps help people find a place for a standard lease. SubZona is for untraditional leases, connecting the natural gaps in student schedules.

PlatformiOS · Android
FocusMarketplace & trust design

Problem
The Problem

The lease calendar doesn't match the school calendar

A 12-month lease can outlast a student's time on campus. Someone leaving to study abroad may need a tenant for just one semester, while an incoming exchange student needs a room for those same months.

"I need a place for four months. Every listing site is built around a full year lease."

Going abroad: students studying abroad for a semester are stuck paying rent on an empty room, breaking their lease outright, or handing their apartment to a stranger off Craigslist with no way to verify who they are.

Coming to campus: exchange students, transfers, and interns need a short stay that lines up with their arrival and departure dates.

Where students look now

Zillow and apartment listings cover a broad rental market, focusing more on long-term leases. Students still need to find a lease that fits their semester.

Airbnb supports monthly stays, but its booking model centers on available accommodation rather than pairing students leaving campus with those arriving.

UA’s off-campus housing portal is a closer alternative: it already includes sublets and roommate search. SubZona would need to make matching overlapping semester dates easier than browsing those listings.

SubZona’s focus: turn opposite travel plans into a match. A student leaving Tucson for spring can connect with someone arriving for that same term, with exact dates checked before they agree.

Sources: Zillow short-term listings · Airbnb monthly stays · UA housing

TARGET MARKET
Who It's For

Two students, opposite problem, same semester

SubZona works because it's anchored to one matched pair: someone leaving, and someone arriving. Every feature exists to optimize this matching system that naturally exists on college campuses.

Outbound

Jordan

Junior · Business · Studying in Barcelona, Spring

Signed a 12-month lease before deciding to go abroad. Doesn't want to break it or pay rent on an empty room, and doesn't want to hand his apartment off to a stranger.

"I just need someone to take over my place for the Spring semester."

Maya

Exchange student · Arriving Spring, one semester only

Never been to Tucson. Every apartment site wants a year-long signature; every Facebook group is unverified strangers with no real dates attached.

"I don't want a full lease. I just need something for the Spring that's close to campus."
How it works
How It Works

Built around the term, not the date

Every listing and search is built around Spring, Summer, or Fall, the unit students actually live in. Once two people are ready to talk, the platform steps back.

.edu to get in

No verified university email, no listing, no browsing.

One page, two intents

Home screen asks one question: find a place, or list one.

Term-first search

Filters lead with semester and length of stay, not a generic map.

Request, then connect

Sending a request shares verified contact info directly. SubZona doesn't host the conversation on the platform.

plan
Establishing the Direction

The action plan

A two-sided marketplace lives or dies on its first few hundred matches. V1 is scoped to get a listing posted and a request accepted, nothing more.

V1.0 · Launch

Post a listing, accept a request

Start once verified students can post a listing, accept a request, and exchange contact details.

  • .edu verification
  • List a place (dates, price, photos)
  • Browse + filter by term
  • Verified contact exchange
V1.1 · Trust & reputation

Reputation after the fact

After the first semester, review how many listings found tenants before adding these features.

  • Ratings after a completed sublease
  • Photo ID verification badge
  • Sublease agreement is satisfied
V2.0 · Beyond one campus

New campuses, deeper search

Expand after two semesters of consistent matches and returning users at U of A.

  • Roommate matching
  • AI agent for search optimization
  • Beyond U of A

Decisions & Trade-offs

The features

Keep the app focused on finding a match.

No in-app payments

Students arrange rent payments directly. SubZona focuses on matching, not processing money.

No in-app messaging

Once a request is accepted, students exchange verified contact details and talk directly.

.edu required to join

A verified university email keeps access tied to the campus community.

Term-first, not date-first search

Start with Spring, Summer, or Fall, then confirm exact dates to check the overlap.

WIREFRAMES
The Flow

Three screens start to finish

Sign up, decide what you're looking for, and either browse or list.

Pick a path

One question, two intents

Find a Sublease, or List My Place. Everything after this screen is written for whichever one you picked.

Find a sublease

Filter by term, not just price

Move-in date, length of stay, and distance to campus lead the filter bar, since those are what actually matter for a one-semester stay.

List your place

Dates first, details second

Listing a place starts with the term (Spring, Summer, or Fall) before anything about price or photos.

Next steps
Roadmap

From one campus to a real marketplace


Phase 1

Prove the loop

Launch at U of A for one semester cycle. Get to a real fill rate before adding anything else.

Phase 2

Deepen trust

ID verification badges, post-sublease ratings, and stronger reporting make the match itself more trustworthy, not the transaction.

Phase 3

Expand through partners

Partner with university housing and study-abroad teams to reach students leaving and arriving each semester.

A possible exit: after proving repeatable matches across campuses, explore an acquisition by an established student-housing platform. The value would be campus partnerships and a matching system students already use. This is a potential path, not a committed buyer or outcome.
